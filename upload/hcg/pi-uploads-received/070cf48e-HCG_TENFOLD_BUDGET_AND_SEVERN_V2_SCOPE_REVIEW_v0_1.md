## Decision

The proposed *necessary dimension inequalities* are correct under the genus-zero, nontrivial absolutely irreducible coefficient-representation hypotheses. The stronger claim that Severn's method is **provably capped at split eightfolds** is **NOT ESTABLISHED**; a representation-theoretic inference improperly treats groups containing a reflection as groups generated by reflections. v2 differs materially from v1 and cannot inherit v1 scientific review grades without targeted successor audit.

## 1. Verified mathematical budget [SESSION — ALG]

Let `d = dim_K sigma`, `N` be the number of **nontrivial** branch elements of a connected Galois cover of P^1, and let the parabolic multiplicity space have K-dimension `2n`. Assume no coefficient invariants and the parabolic dimension formula. Put `c_j = rank_K(1 - sigma(g_j))`. Then

`sum_j c_j = 2n + 2d`, `N - 3 >= n^2` is necessary for dominance of an n^2-dimensional Weil-type moduli cell, and `c_j >= 1`. Consequently:

`d >= (n^2 - 2n + 3)/2`.

For `n=5`, `d>=9`. If **none** of the local monodromies is a reflection (all `c_j>=2`), then `2N<=sum c_j` and

`d >= n^2 - n + 3 = 23` for `n=5`.

More precise: `sum_j(c_j-1) = 2d+2n-N <= 2d+2n-n^2-3`. For `n=5`, `sum_j(c_j-1) <= 2d-18`, so there are at most `2d-18` non-reflection branch elements. At the minimal `d=9`, all local elements are reflections and `N=28`; because the tuple generates G, G is then a complex reflection group. A non-real-character, irreducible nine-dimensional **reflection-generated** group is excluded by the Shephard–Todd classification plus the imprimitive/monomial exclusion *provided that exclusion's hypotheses hold*. This does not exclude d>=10.

For base genus one, and the analogous irreducibility/no-invariant conditions, `2n = sum c_j >=N`; the moduli of genus-one curves with N unordered marked points has dimension N after translation and varying the elliptic curve. Thus `n^2<=N<=2n`, implying `n<=2`. Note that the genus-one argument is a separate extension of the P^1 class datum and depends on these moduli assumptions.

The `G34` reflection representation has `d=6`. At `N=20`, `2n=20-12=8`, `n=4`, `N-3=17>=16`; `N=22` would give `n=5`, `19<25` (explicitly stated by Severn v2, §9).

## 2. Critical overreach in the proposed cap [SESSION — SCIENTIFIC REVIEW]

The classification of **primitive complex reflection groups** does not classify every primitive finite subgroup of GL_d(K) that merely **contains** a reflection. A finite group with non-real character may contain real reflections while requiring non-reflection elements to generate the whole group.

A concrete group-theoretic diagnostic (not a valid (L)-datum): For `d>=9`, take the standard irreducible reflection representation `V` of `S_{d+1}` over Q and enlarge the matrix group to `G = <S_{d+1}, omega I_d>` over `K=Q(omega)`. Its representation is irreducible (and its underlying reflection representation is primitive), has character field K because `tr(omega I_d)=d omega`, and contains rank-one rational reflections. Yet the whole group is **not** generated by these reflections. This invalidates the inference “a primitive G containing a reflection must be an irreducible complex reflection group.” It does **not** construct a Severn datum: central scalar classes are non-real, so the real-class (L) route and target signature may fail.

The v1 §8 argument excludes **monomial representations** for `n>=4` via a moduli-dimension count `<=3n<n^2`; it does not exclude *all irreducible representations of every imprimitive reflection group*, only those to which monomial induction applies. Any extension of this exclusion requires explicit hypotheses.

Therefore: `d>=9`, `d>=23 if no reflections`, and the narrow d=9 obstruction are checked; the blanket impossibility of split tenfolds or non-split eightfolds via tenfold compensation is **UNPROVEN**. The user's feasibility-first research direction remains sensible.

## 3. Boundary Witt-rank corollary [SESSION — CHECKED, NON-NOVEL]

If all `N_iN_j=0`, the `N_i` are skew for a nondegenerate skew-Hermitian S, and `I=sum im N_i` has K-dimension r, then I is isotropic, `I^perp` is in each kernel, and the Hermitian forms `beta_i(x,y)=S(x,N_i y)` factor through the r-dimensional quotient. Thus `dim_Q span{N_i}<=r^2<=w_K(S)^2`. For a nonsplit (4,4) Hermitian space of K-dimension 8, Landherr gives Witt index 3, giving `<=9<16`. This is the previously checked HCG laminar bound with the Witt-index observation; Severn v2 Proposition 8.5 / Remark 8.6 explicitly states the split-only conclusion, so there is no novelty claim.
