### Full-plane algebraicity

The key object is the rational rank-two local system

\\[ \mathcal W=\bigwedge_K^{2n}R^1f\_\*\mathbb Q \subset R^{2n}f\_\*\mathbb Q. \\]

Lemma 6.1 constructs the locus where the entire fibre \\(\mathcal W_t\\) lies in the rational span of algebraic cycle classes.

The relative Hilbert-scheme argument works with finite tuples of cycles, not one class. For each tuple, their cohomology classes form a flat rational subspace \\(F\\) after passage to an appropriate connected parameter space. Inclusion \\(\mathcal W\subseteq F\\) is locally constant. Properness of the Hilbert schemes makes the resulting images closed algebraic subsets, and there are only countably many relevant tuples.

Hence

\\[ M\_{\mathrm{alg}}=\bigcup\_{j\ge1}Z_j, \qquad Z_j\subseteq M\ \text{closed algebraic}. \\]

This addresses the entire Weil plane.

### From an open subset to every member

[THM] Lemma 6.2 uses the Baire property: if a nonempty Euclidean open subset of the connected, irreducible Weil moduli variety \\(M\\) lies in \\(M\_{\mathrm{alg}}\\), one of the \\(Z_j\\) contains an open subset and therefore equals \\(M\\).

Theorem 7.1 obtains that open set through full period-map rank \\(n^2\\). Landherr classification matches the rational Hermitian spaces; commensurable lattices yield \\(K\\)-linear isogenies. These preserve algebraicity of the Weil plane.

Thus the chain is

\\[ \begin{aligned} (L)&\Rightarrow\text{cycles at Hurwitz fibres},\\\ (D)+d(S)&\Rightarrow\text{open image in }M(K,n,d),\\\ \text{Hilbert/Baire}&\Rightarrow M\_{\mathrm{alg}}=M,\\\ \text{Landherr/isogeny}&\Rightarrow\text{every object of class }d. \end{aligned} \\]

## Priority B7 — sixfold compensation

VERIFIED as a conditional reduction

[THM] Severn Draft v0.5, Lemma 5.1 and Proposition 5.2 (pp. 10–11), give a valid product-and-contraction argument, conditional on algebraicity of the full Weil plane for every eightfold in the chosen discriminant cell.

### Discriminant control

Let \\(A\\) be an arbitrary polarized Weil sixfold with discriminant \\([t]\\), where \\(t<0\\). For \\(n=4\\), take

\\[ S=E\times\bar E \\]

with product-polarization degrees \\((1,m)\\). Lemma 5.1 yields

\\[ \operatorname{disc}(S)=[-m],\qquad \operatorname{disc}(A\times S)=[-tm]. \\]

Choose \\(m>0\\) such that \\([-tm]=[1]\\). This is always possible: writing \\(t=-a/b\\), with positive integers \\(a,b\\), take \\(m=ab\\). Then

\\[ -tm=a^2=N\_{K/\mathbb Q}(a). \\]

Thus \\(A\times S\\) belongs to the split eightfold discriminant class, including when \\(A\\) itself has nonsplit discriminant.

This construction is compatible with the \\(K\\)-action and gives signature \\((4,4)\\).

### Recovery of both rational Weil classes

[ALG] The \\(K\\)-exterior-power identity is

\\[ \bigwedge_K^8 H^1(A\times S,\mathbb Q) \simeq \bigwedge_K^6 H^1(A,\mathbb Q) \otimes_K \bigwedge_K^2 H^1(S,\mathbb Q). \\]

Choose a nonzero rational algebraic Weil class \\(\xi\\) on \\(A\times S\\) and a nonzero rational divisor class \\(y\in W_K(S)\\). Fibre integration defines an algebraic class

\\[ z=\operatorname{pr}\_{A\*} \left(\xi\smile\operatorname{pr}\_S^\*y\right) \in W_K(A). \\]

The determinant-line pairings show \\(z\neq0\\).

One rational class is insufficient. The proof obtains a second by applying an endomorphism \\(\alpha\in\mathcal O_K\\) to \\(S\\) before contraction. Under the two \\(K\\)-embeddings, the surviving coefficient changes by \\(\bar\iota(\alpha)^2\\).

For \\(K=\mathbb Q(w)\\), take \\(\alpha=1\\) and \\(\alpha=w\\), where \\(w^2+w+1=0\\). Then

\\[ \bar\iota(w)^2=w\notin\mathbb Q. \\]

Therefore the two rational classes have distinct nonrational coefficient ratios and are \\(\mathbb Q\\)-linearly independent. Since

\\[ \dim\_{\mathbb Q}W_K(A)=2, \\]

they span the full Weil plane.

An independent exact script verifies the discriminant construction and the rank-two coefficient test. As a negative control, using \\(\alpha=1\\) and \\(\alpha=2\\) yields a rational ratio and correctly fails to produce rank two.

Conclusion: all sixfold discriminants are covered, provided Priority B6 supplies algebraicity at every eightfold, including the special product \\(A\times S\\). A merely generic eightfold assertion would be insufficient.

Powers: no conclusion is drawn for arbitrary powers here. The separate powers result requires \\(Hg(A)=SU_H\\) for the particular sixfold, as stated in Theorem 2.11.
