## [SESSION — THM (conditional)] Invariant-theoretic all-powers reduction for full SU

Let `A` be an abelian sixfold with a Weil-type action of `K = Q(sqrt(-3))`, with complex Hodge group `Hg(A)_C = SL(V)`, `dim_C V = 6`, acting on `H^1(A,C) = V \oplus V^*` in the standard/contragredient way. If the complete rational Weil plane `W_K(A)` is algebraic, then the rational Hodge conjecture holds for all powers `A^r`, `r >= 1`.

Proof. As an `SL(V)`-module, `H^1(A^r,C) = (V \otimes C^r) \oplus (V^* \otimes C^r)`. By the First Fundamental Theorem for `SL(V)`, invariants in the ambient tensor algebra are generated by identity contractions `V \otimes V^* -> C` and the volume tensors `det V`, `det V^*`. Exterior antisymmetrization gives all invariants in the cohomology algebra. Identity contractions correspond to rational divisor/Poincare classes. The two volume tensors are the complexifications of the rational Weil plane. The coefficient of every mixed placement among copies of `A` is obtained by polynomial polarization of pullbacks along integer addition maps `A^r -> A`, `(x_i) |-> \sum t_i x_i`, and, where appropriate, by algebraic `K`-endomorphism correspondences. Rational descent from `K` preserves algebraicity. Hence all rational Hodge invariants on any power are algebraic. QED.

This is a classical invariant-theory reduction, not a novel proof of algebraicity of the Weil plane and **not** a proof for special members with proper Hodge subgroup.

## [SESSION — NUM] One exact checkpoint in the CM-sixfold special-member argument

The 20 balanced sign vectors `s in {+1,-1}^6` with three plus and three minus entries have pairwise dot products only in `{-6,-2,2,6}`; no two such vectors are orthogonal. This is independently enumerable (see `check_balanced_vectors.py`). It verifies only the stated finite combinatorial sublemma of the rStation source, not the CM Hodge-torus rank-five theorem or its endomorphism assumptions.

## [SESSION — NUM] Cyclic CM sanity test and the simplicity guard

For a model CM Galois action `C6 x C2` on the six complex-conjugate embedding pairs, the 20 balanced sign types split by the exact rational ranks of their cyclic Galois spans:

| Rational span rank | Number | Nontrivial CM-type stabilizer |
|---:|---:|---|
| 5 | 12 | No |
| 3 | 6 | Yes (a shift, optionally followed by conjugation) |
| 1 | 2 | Yes |

This test confirms that **balanced signature alone does not imply rank five**; the simple/primitive CM-type hypothesis is substantive. In this cyclic model, each lower-rank case has a nontrivial stabilizer and is therefore an imprimitive CM type. See `check_cyclic_cm_signs.py`. This is only a model check, not a verification of the source's general Galois-stable rank argument for all CM sextic data.
