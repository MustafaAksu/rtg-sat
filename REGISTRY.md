# Registry — satellite studies in `rtg-sat`

A study is in `rtg-sat` only when it has a row here. Rows are added or changed by PI decision. "Not set" means no decision has been recorded yet; it is not a default.

**Owners.** The owners of a satellite study are the AI sessions of the earlier conversations in which that study was carried out (PI decision, 4 Nov 2026). Each row names them once those conversations are identified; until then the row says "to be identified".
