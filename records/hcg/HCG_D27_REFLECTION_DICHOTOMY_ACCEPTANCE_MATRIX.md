## 0. Conventions

- **K** = ℚ(√−3); a **class** is an element of ℚ^×/N(K^×).
- A **datum** (Γ, σ; g₁, …, g_N): Γ ⊂ GL_d(K) finite, σ its absolutely irreducible action on V = K^d, a product-one generating tuple of effective elements, c_j = rank(1 − g_j) ≥ 1, ε = Σ(c_j − 1).
- **(H)**: base of genus 0; character field of σ exactly K; no fixed vector; dim_K M_σ = 8 with signature (4,4); **(D)**: period rank 16. Under (H): Σc_j = 2d + 8, N ≥ 19 and ε ≤ 2d − 11 (`HCG-ALG-DIM-BUDGET`, with effective points only by step P6 of the induced bound; M-D6 §1.2, R1–R3).
- A **reflection** is an element with c = 1, of any finite order. **W_R** is the subgroup of Γ generated by all reflections of Γ.
- "Split" means class [1] of the parabolic cup form (layer L1/L2 of ledger §6.24 (a)). The reading for polarized abelian varieties is a separate conditional sentence (§4.4).

## 1. `HCG-LEM-NONSPLIT8-NORMAL-REFL`

### 1.1 Statement

Let (Γ, σ; g_j) satisfy (H) and let Γ contain a reflection. Then:

- **(S) Structure.** W_R is normal and nontrivial, V = V₁ ⊕ … ⊕ V_k orthogonally, with the V_i defined over K, W_R = ΠW_i, each W_i an irreducible reflection group on V_i. Γ permutes the V_i transitively, so dim V_i = r and d = rk.
- **(C) Monomial normalizer.** If k = 1 and W_R is conjugate to G(m, p, d) with m ≥ 2 and d ≥ 5, then Γ permutes the d coordinate lines of W_R, so σ is monomial over ℂ.
- **(B) Real Weyl normalizer.** If k = 1 and W_R is a Weyl group of type A_d (d ≥ 2), E₆, E₇ or E₈, then Γ ⊂ W_R·μ₆, and the tuple contains at least two elements λw with λ non-real. Hence ε ≥ 2(⌈d/2⌉ − 1) for E₆, E₇, E₈ and ε ≥ 2(d − ⌊(d+1)/3⌋ − 1) for A_d.
- **(D) Block budget.** If k ≥ 2: for g inducing the permutation π of the blocks, rank(1 − g) = r·ind(π) + Σ_a rank(1 − P_a), with P_a the return map on the cycle a. Hence ε ≥ 2(r − 1)(k − 1 + γ), with γ the genus of the k-sheeted cover Y.

### 1.2 Proof chain

| Step | Content | Uses | Remark |
|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | The reflections of Γ form a conjugation-stable set, so W_R is normal. Its fixed space is Γ-stable, hence zero | absolute irreducibility | — |
| S2 | Join two reflection lines when they are not orthogonal for the Γ-invariant positive Hermitian form. The components span mutually orthogonal K-subspaces V_i; the reflections of one component act irreducibly on its span and trivially on the others | the roots of reflections in GL_d(K) are K-vectors | the same graph argument works over ℂ, so each W_i is absolutely irreducible on V_i |
| S3 | Γ permutes the reflection lines, hence the components; one orbit would span a proper invariant subspace unless the action is transitive | irreducibility | — |
| C1 | The reflecting hyperplanes of G(m, p, d) are x_i = ζx_j (ζ ∈ μ_m) and, if p < m, x_i = 0. A line with z zero coordinates lies on at most f(z) = m·C(z,2) + C(d−z,2) + ηz of them (η = 1 if p < m, else 0), with equality M = m·C(d−1,2) + η(d−1) at a coordinate line | direct count | NR-SRC wrote "+z"; corrected to "+ηz" (D-13, wording only) |
| C2 | f is convex in z, and M − f(0) = ((d−1)/2)(m(d−2) − d + 2η) > 0, M − f(d−2) = m(d−2) − 1 + η > 0 for m ≥ 2, d ≥ 5. So the coordinate lines are exactly the lines on the largest number of hyperplanes. Γ normalizes W_R, permutes its hyperplanes, and so permutes the coordinate lines | C1 | at d = 4, m = 2 there is a tie (triality of D₄); not relevant for d ≥ 5 |
| B1 | g ∈ Γ preserves the invariant form h and maps root lines to root lines. With simple roots scaled so that h(α_i, α_j) = −1 along the diagram: gα_i = c_iβ_i with β_i roots, \|c_i\| = 1, and c_i c̄_j h(β_i, β_j) = −1 with h(β_i, β_j) real forces c_i = ±c_j. So g/c₁ maps the root lattice to itself: g ∈ Aut(Φ)·ℂ^× | W_R irreducible, simply laced, connected diagram | NR-SRC argued through "α_i + α_j is a root"; this row gives the direct form. Same conclusion |
| B2 | Aut(Φ) = W for E₇, E₈ and W × ⟨−1⟩ for A_d (d ≥ 2) and E₆. So Γ ⊂ W_R·ℂ^× | classical | **import**: automorphism groups of these root systems |
| B3 | The scalars are roots of unity in K, so in μ₆. The scalar modulo ±1 is a homomorphism on Γ. If it is trivial, all traces are rational, against (H). So it is nontrivial, and a product-one generating tuple has at least two entries with non-real scalar | character field exactly K | the field hypothesis is essential here |
| B4 | For λ non-real and w real, Fix(λw) is the λ̄-eigenspace of w, of dimension ≤ ⌊d/2⌋; for w the standard part of a permutation of d + 1 letters it is ≤ ⌊(d+1)/3⌋ | linear algebra | — |
| D1 | On a cycle of ℓ blocks a fixed vector is determined by its first component v with P_a v = v. Subtracting from rℓ gives the identity | linear algebra | the exact identity is ChatGPT's sharpening (NR-REV §7) |
| D2 | Σ_j ind(π_j) = 2(k − 1) + 2γ (Riemann–Hurwitz for the transitive product-one tuple in S_k). Each block-moving entry has c_j − 1 ≥ r·ind(π_j) − 1 ≥ (r − 1)·ind(π_j) | Riemann–Hurwitz | — |

### 1.3 The exclusions recorded with the lemma (addenda 3–4)

Under (H), by (B), (C), (D), the Shephard–Todd list and the monomial exclusion: no datum for d = 7, 8, 9, 11; at d = 10 only two imprimitive types survive the budget (2 blocks of rank 5, 5 blocks of rank 2), and both are excluded by the induced period bound (bounds 15 and 9). **These are special cases of Theorem R (§3)** and need no separate row.

## 2. Lemma F — the symmetric-group type (no RID in the log)

### 2.1 Statement (in the scope that Theorem R needs)

Let (Γ, σ; g_j) satisfy (H) with k = 1 and W_R = S_{d+1} acting on V by its standard d-dimensional reflection representation, d ≥ 2. Then Γ = S_{d+1} × μ_m with m ∈ {3, 6}, σ = std ⊗ χ for the tautological character χ of μ_m, and

  rank Per_σ ≤ 15 < 16.

So no such datum satisfies (D): the case does not occur under (H).

### 2.2 Proof chain

| Step | Content | Uses |
|---|---|---|
| F1 | By (B), Γ ⊂ S_{d+1}·μ₆. The decomposition g = μp is unique, because no nontrivial scalar lies in S_{d+1} (d ≥ 2). Since S_{d+1} ⊂ Γ, the scalars μ lie in Γ: Γ = S_{d+1} × μ_m, and m ∈ {3, 6} because non-real scalars occur (B3) | §1 (B) |
| F2 | σ ⊕ χ = (std ⊕ 1) ⊗ χ is the permutation representation twisted by χ, that is Ind from S_d × μ_m of the rank-one character χ′ = 1 ⊠ χ | representation theory |
| F3 | Let t be the number of entries with μ_j ≠ 1. M_χ is the parabolic cohomology of a rank-one local system on P¹ with t nontrivial local monodromies: dimension t − 2. So H¹_par(Y, L_χ′) ≅ M_σ ⊕ M_χ has dimension 6 + t = 2γ − 2 + s′, with Y the (d+1)-sheeted cover and s′ its number of effective points | `HCG-THM-INDUCED-PERIOD-BOUND`, P4–P7 with r = 1 |
| F4 | M_σ is a canonical Hodge summand, so its period map factors through the moduli of (Y, D_χ′): rank Per_σ ≤ 3γ − 3 + s′ = γ + 5 + t. From s′ ≥ 0: γ ≤ 4 + t/2, so rank Per_σ ≤ 9 + 3t/2 | same theorem, P6, profile (ii) suffices |
| F5 | At least two μ_j are non-real (F1, product one), each with c_j − 1 ≥ A = d − ⌊(d+1)/3⌋ − 1 ≥ (2d − 4)/3. Every other entry with μ_j ≠ 1 has c_j − 1 ≥ B = d − ⌊(d+1)/2⌋ − 1 ≥ (d − 3)/2, and A ≥ B | §1 (B4) |
| F6 | If t ≥ 5 then ε ≥ 2A + 3B ≥ (17d − 43)/6 > 2d − 11 for every d, against the budget. So t ≤ 4 and rank Per_σ ≤ 15 | budget |

### 2.3 Scope

- Lemma F is used here only for Γ containing S_{d+1} in its standard reflection representation. R-SRC also names A_{d+1} × μ_m and std ⊗ sgn ⊗ χ. ChatGPT accepted those only "when the indicated scalar-character decomposition is actually supplied; mere abstract projective isomorphism is not a substitute" (R-REV §3). **They are not needed for Theorem R and are not proposed here.**
- ChatGPT's stronger statement (t = 2, rank ≤ 12) and the "triple lemma" of addendum 6 are later material and are not part of this matrix.

## 3. Theorem R — the reflection dichotomy (no RID in the log)

### 3.1 Statement

Let (Γ, σ; g_j) satisfy (H). If Γ contains a complex reflection, then the discriminant class of the parabolic cup form is [1].

Equivalently: a nonsplit datum under (H) has a reflection-free image. The theorem says nothing about data without dominance; the cyclic C₆ separator of Δ27 is nonsplit, contains reflections, and is not dominant.

### 3.2 Proof chain

| Step | Case | Argument | Uses |
|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | — | Structure (S): V = ⊕V_i, k blocks of rank r | §1 |
| T2 | r = 1 | σ is monomial; period rank ≤ 12 < 16; excluded by (D) | `HCG-THM-INDUCED-PERIOD-BOUND`, r = 1 |
| T3 | k ≥ 2, r ≥ 2 | The blocks are K-defined, so σ is induced over K from τ = the action of the stabilizer of V₁. Lemma E: the τ-datum satisfies (H) in dimension r ≥ 6, has the same class, and its reflection subgroup contains W₁, irreducible on V₁. Replace the datum by the τ-datum; it has k = 1 | `HCG-LEM-INDUCING-DATUM` (M-E), parts (ii)–(iv) |
| T4 | k = 1, dimension 6 | The class is [1] | `HCG-COR-D6-NONSPLIT-NOGO` (M-D6): every datum of dimension 6 meeting the target is split |
| T5 | k = 1, dimension d′ ≥ 7 | W_R is an irreducible reflection group of rank d′ ≥ 7. Shephard–Todd: G(m, p, d′) with m ≥ 2, type A_{d′}, E₇ (d′ = 7), E₈ (d′ = 8) | **import**: Shephard–Todd classification |
| T6 | G(m, p, d′), m ≥ 2 | (C): σ is monomial over ℂ; excluded as in T2. The coordinate lines need not be K-defined; profile (ii) of the induced bound does not need them to be | §1 (C); induced bound, profile (ii) |
| T7 | A_{d′} | Lemma F: period rank ≤ 15; excluded by (D) | §2 |
| T8 | E₇, E₈ | (B): ε ≥ 2(⌈d′/2⌉ − 1) = 6, against the budget 2d′ − 11 = 3 and 5 | §1 (B) |
| T9 | — | In every case the datum is split, or it violates (D) and so does not satisfy (H) | — |

### 3.3 A change with respect to the sources (for the reviewer)

In R-SRC and R-REV the groups of rank 6 are treated one by one: E₆ by the budget (ε ≥ 4 > 1), and G₃₄ by showing that the image is G₃₄, which uses the statement that the linear normalizer of G₃₄ acts by inner automorphisms modulo scalars (**Marin–Michel**, an import read by ChatGPT only).

This matrix replaces both by step T4: `HCG-COR-D6-NONSPLIT-NOGO` already covers **every** datum of dimension 6 that meets the target, whatever its group. So the Marin–Michel import is no longer needed for Theorem R, and the classification is used only in rank ≥ 7.

This rearrangement has been checked by nobody but its author. If the reviewer rejects it, T4 is replaced by the two rank-6 cases as in the sources, with Marin–Michel as a named import.

## 4. The corollary for d ≤ 14 (no RID in the log)

### 4.1 Statement

Under (H), a datum with d ≤ 14 has split discriminant class. So no nonsplit datum under (H) exists for d ≤ 14, and a nonsplit datum under (H) has a reflection-free image, all c_j ≥ 2, d ≥ 15 and N ≤ d + 4.

### 4.2 Proof

If every c_j ≥ 2, then 2N ≤ Σc_j = 2d + 8, and N ≥ 19 gives d ≥ 15. So for d ≤ 14 the tuple contains a reflection, and Theorem R applies.

### 4.3 Scope correction on record (D-14)

Claude's sentence in R-SRC, "by Lemma E one may assume σ primitive", was too strong. Lemma E descends only through K-defined block systems, so it gives a representative that is minimal for induction over K, not one that is primitive over ℂ. Found by ChatGPT (example: AGL(1,7) × μ₃ in degree 6); conceded by Claude. That sentence is **not** part of this corollary.

### 4.4 The polarized reading (a separate, conditional sentence)

If the class of the cup form is the discriminant of the induced polarized factor (Severn v2 Prop 7.1, unrefereed) and Weil's criterion is used, then no datum under (H) with d ≤ 14 gives a nonsplit Weil eightfold.

### 4.5 What the corollary does not say

- Nothing for d ≥ 15: the open item on nonsplit eightfolds stays open, and the later bounds of Δ27 (d ≥ 25, …, d ≥ 53) are not reviewed here.
- Nothing outside (H): other fields, higher-genus bases, infinite monodromy, or families that are not Hurwitz families of this kind.
- It is not a statement that nonsplit Weil eightfolds do not exist, and not a statement about the Hodge conjecture.
