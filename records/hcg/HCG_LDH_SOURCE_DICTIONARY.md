## 3. (H0)–(H4) — the boundary hypotheses of Severn's Prop 4.2

These are five conditions on a boundary (degeneration) datum: a pants decomposition with its nilpotent operators N_j and forms β_j. Together they are Severn's criterion for (D).

| Label | HCG restatement of the role (PO-REVIEW, lines 135–138) | HCG's instance on the W5 certificate (LEDGER §6.14, lines 644–648) |
|---|---|---|
| (H0) | "(H0) states a Clemens–Schmid count." | "(H0) Every boundary product is 1; the pants contributions vanish." |
| (H1) | "(H1)–(H3) make the limiting MHS Hodge–Tate with W₀ = W₁ = I." | "(H1) N_jN_k = 0 for all 289 ordered pairs." |
| (H2) | same | "(H2) I = Σ im N_j has K-dimension 4, is S-isotropic, and N_j(I) = 0." |
| (H3) | same | "(H3) Every β_j is negative semidefinite, and Σβ_j has signature (0,4,0)." |
| (H4) | "(H4) the N_j span 𝔫_I, so the orbit map has full rank n² = 16 = dim of the SU(4,4) domain." | "(H4) dim_ℚ span N_j = dim_K span N_j = 16." |

**Further points:**
- **Primary source:** Severn, `hexagon:2610.00387v1` Prop 4.2. The W5 column is an instance for n = 4, on the certificate `g34_cert_tuple5_balanced.json` (`172766a9…eafb952e`); the same five checks are in GATE-BC lines 59–63.
- **A finite certificate is only that.** SG-6: W5/W6/W7 matrix data certify their finite assertions; geometric meaning needs the G34-D bridge (LEDGER §6.15).
- **Equivalent forms recorded by HCG:**
  - `HCG-ALG-H4-BOUND` (LEDGER line 798): under (H2), (H4) ⇔ the β_j span Herm₄(K).
  - `HCG-THM-EDGEFLUX` (LEDGER lines 98 and 806): for pair-containing decompositions (H0)–(H3) are automatic and (H4) ⇔ the edge forms span Herm(L). Its novelty is limited by prior art (Alexeev–Donagi–Farkas–Izadi–Ortega).
  - `HCG-ALG-LAMINAR-H0H4` (LEDGER line 828): for laminar interval trees in a fixed marking, under the stated premises, Prop 4.2 reduces to (H0) + (H4).
- **Change in Severn's v2 (pending, Δ26; not part of v0.2).** V2-AUDIT line 61: "(H0) removed from the criterion": in `hexagon:2610.00387v2`, Prop 8.5 needs (H1)–(H4) only, and (H0) follows from Lemma 8.3 with Clemens–Schmid. A consumer must say which version's hypothesis list it uses.

## 4. (H) — the standing hypotheses of the HHO opening note (a different object)

"(H)" without an index is **not** Severn's (H0)–(H4). It is the list of standing hypotheses of the nonsplit-eightfold work (Δ27), collected in HHO-NOTE §2, lines 53–65:

1. "Γ is finite and absolutely irreducible over K, with character field exactly K. It preserves the positive Hermitian form h₀ and has no fixed vectors."
2. "The Weil part M_σ = H¹_par(P¹, σ) has dim_K M_σ = 8 (n = 4) and signature (4, 4)."
3. "**(D) dominance:** the period map has rank 16."
4. The budget relations, with c_j = rank(1 − g_j) (paraphrased from lines 60–63): Σ_j c_j = 2d + 8; N_eff ≥ 19; ε := Σ_j (c_j − 1) ≤ 2d − 11.

and, at line 65: (L) "is a **separate** hypothesis and is not part of (H)."

**Status.** This (H) is pending material (Δ27, Δ28) and HHO-track source evidence. It is not part of `hcg-v0.2`. The d-bounds of Δ27 (nonsplit ⇒ d ≥ 53) are stated under this (H). They are not to be merged with, or set against, other dimension results until both hypothesis lists are compared item by item.
