# HCG scope note — from "Weil classes are algebraic" to the Hodge conjecture (Claude)

**Date:** 12 November 2026. **By:** the Claude HCG owner session. **For review by:** the ChatGPT HCG owner session. **Decision:** PI.

**Status:** owner-session note, PROPOSAL, revision 2 (one wording correction from the co-owner review of PR #61). Nothing here is adopted; `hcg-v0.5` governs and the ledger is not changed. The note states **what would follow if** Severn's Theorems A and B hold. They are CLAIMED, not established, and this note does not change that.

**Why this note.** "The Weil classes of A are algebraic" and "the Hodge conjecture holds for A" are different statements. The ledger audits the first. This note fixes, for each kind of variety, how far the first reaches towards the second, so that no later text reads more into the chain than it carries.

**Sources.** Severn v2 (hexagon:2610.00387v2, held PDF `ee285c82…`): Theorems A, B, C (§1), Theorem 11.2 and its proof (§11), read as text extraction on 12 Nov 2026. The classical results below are stated from general knowledge of the literature and from Severn's citations (van Geemen's survey, Thm 6.11–6.12; Weil; Moonen–Zarhin). **They were not re-read for this note**; a source check would go through LIT. Markman's results are as recorded in ledger §6.6.

## 1. The implication, and where it holds

Let (A, ι, E) be a polarized abelian 2n-fold of Weil type for an imaginary quadratic field K, with Hermitian form H and Hodge group Hg(A) ⊆ SU_H.

| # | Statement | Kind | State in HCG |
|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | In general, "Weil classes algebraic" does **not** imply "all Hodge classes algebraic" | — | a member with a smaller Hodge group **can have** further Hodge classes, which the Weil-class statement does not reach |
| S2 | If Hg(A) = SU_H, the Hodge ring of A is generated by divisor classes and the Weil classes | classical (Weil; van Geemen's survey) | import, not re-read. The invariant-theory reason: H¹(A, ℂ) = W ⊕ W^*, SU_H(ℂ) = SL(W), and the invariants in the exterior algebra are generated by the pairing (degree 2) and the two determinants (degree 2n) |
| S3 | Hence, if Hg(A) = SU_H and the Weil classes of A are algebraic, **the Hodge conjecture holds for A** | S2 and the Lefschetz (1,1) theorem | follows from S2 |
| S4 | Hg(A) = SU_H holds for the very general member of each moduli space: outside a countable union of proper closed subvarieties | classical (van Geemen, Thm 6.11, as cited by Severn) | import, not re-read |
| S5 | If Hg(A) = SU_H and the Weil classes of A are algebraic, the Hodge conjecture holds for **every power** A^m | Severn v2 Theorem 11.2 (invariant theory for SL(W) on several copies; Weil classes pulled back along homomorphisms A^m → A) | **not audited in HCG** (OPEN 10). Unrefereed |
| S6 | For abelian varieties of dimension ≤ 5, Hodge classes reduce to divisors and Weil classes | Moonen–Zarhin 1999 | ledger §6.6, `HCG-MATH-LIT` |

S2–S4 use no property of ℚ(√−3) and no result of Severn's.

## 2. What the audited chain would give

"Would give" means: **if** Theorem A (split eightfolds) and Theorem B (all sixfolds) of Severn v2 hold.

| Varieties, K = ℚ(√−3) | Weil classes | Full Hodge conjecture | Through |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sixfolds of Weil type, **split** class, Hg = SU_H | established independently (Markman, every K) | follows by S3; independent of Severn | ledger §6.6 |
| Sixfolds of Weil type, **any other** discriminant, Hg = SU_H (the very general member of each cell) | Theorem B (CLAIMED) | **would follow** by S3 | the chain PO-1…PO-6 |
| Sixfolds of Weil type with a **smaller** Hodge group (CM members, products, members with more endomorphisms) | Theorem B (CLAIMED) covers their Weil classes | **does not follow**. These can have further Hodge classes, and nothing in the chain treats them | — |
| **Powers** A^m of a sixfold with Hg = SU_H | — | would follow by S5 (Theorem C) | S5 is unaudited (OPEN 10) |
| Split **eightfolds** of Weil type, Hg = SU_H | Theorem A (CLAIMED) | would follow by S3 | the chain without the compensation step |
| Nonsplit eightfolds | open; nobody claims them | — | ledger §6.25 gives only an obstruction for one construction |
| Abelian sixfolds that are **not** of Weil type for ℚ(√−3), and abelian sixfolds in general | not addressed | not addressed | Severn v2 §12 (type III) is unaudited |

**In one sentence.** If the chain holds, its Hodge-conjecture content is: *the Hodge conjecture for the very general abelian sixfold of Weil type for ℚ(√−3) in each non-split discriminant cell, and for the very general split eightfold* — new cases beyond dimension 5 — and, with the unaudited Theorem C, for all powers of those sixfolds. It is **not** the Hodge conjecture for all abelian sixfolds, and not for the special members of these families.

## 3. Consequences for how HCG words its results

- A ledger row about Weil classes must not say "Hodge conjecture" unless it names the Hodge-group hypothesis (S3) or the dimension bound (S6).
- The special member A × Y of the compensation step is a case in point: its Weil classes are used, and the Hodge conjecture for A × Y is neither used nor obtained.
- §6.24–§6.25 of the ledger are about the class of a cup form in one construction. They carry no Hodge-conjecture content; they say where that construction cannot reach.
- "Very general" is a statement about a complement of countably many proper closed subsets. It gives no explicit example. Whether a given A_σ(C) has Hg = SU_H is a separate question (Severn v2 Remark 3.4 argues it for C outside a countable union of proper analytic subsets).
