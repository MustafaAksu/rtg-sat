## 0. Conventions and the object of the chain

- **K** = ℚ(√−3), ω a primitive cube root of unity, **N** the norm, a **class** an element of ℚ^×/N(K^×). Every positive rational square is a norm, and 3 = N(√−3).
- A **datum** is (G, σ; g₁, …, g_N): a finite group G, an absolutely irreducible representation σ: G → GL_d(K), and a product-one generating tuple, giving a family of G-covers of P¹ with N branch points. c_j = rank(1 − σ(g_j)). A branch point is **effective** if c_j ≥ 1.
- The **target** is: dim_K M_σ = 8 (n = 4), signature (4,4), and period rank 16. Period rank 16 is condition (D) for this target.
- h₀ is the G-invariant positive definite K-Hermitian form on σ. It is unique up to a positive rational factor.

## 1. The reduction to G₃₄ at d = 6 (Δ27 addendum 1, item i; no RID in the log)

### 1.1 Statement

Let (G, σ; g_j) be a datum with d = 6 that meets the target. Then the image σ(G) is generated by complex reflections, and it is conjugate to the Shephard–Todd group G₃₄. The local data are one of:

- **N_eff = 20:** twenty reflections, or
- **N_eff = 19:** eighteen reflections and one element of rank 2.

G itself may be a proper extension of its image.

### 1.2 Proof chain

| Step | Content | Uses | State of what is used |
|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | Σc_j = 2d + 8 = 20 | dimension formula for parabolic cohomology (σ irreducible, no invariants) | classical; in `HCG-ALG-DIM-BUDGET` (§6.23) |
| R2 | Period rank ≤ N_eff − 3, where only effective points count | `HCG-THM-INDUCED-PERIOD-BOUND`, step P6 with H = G and τ = σ: the Hodge structure on M_σ depends only on the points with nontrivial σ-monodromy | §6.24 (b), CHECKED (two AIs) |
| R3 | Hence N_eff ≥ 19 and ε = Σ(c_j − 1) = 20 − N_eff ≤ 1 | R1, R2, period rank 16 | — |
| R4 | N_eff = 20: all σ(g_j) are reflections. N_eff = 19: one has rank 2 and equals the inverse of a product of the eighteen reflections. In both cases σ(G) is generated by reflections | product one; the g_j generate G | — |
| R5 | σ(G) is an irreducible finite complex reflection group of rank 6. By Shephard–Todd these are: G(m, p, 6) with m ≥ 2; the symmetric group S₇ in its 6-dimensional representation; W(E₆) = G₃₅; and G₃₄ | Shephard–Todd classification | **import**, classical and published; not re-derived, tables not re-read |
| R6 | G(m, p, 6) is excluded: its representation is induced from a character of a subgroup of index 6, so the period rank is ≤ 3n = 12 < 16 | `HCG-THM-INDUCED-PERIOD-BOUND`, case r = 1, profile (ii) | §6.24 (b). The sources used Severn v1 §8 here; the ledger theorem replaces it |
| R7 | S₇ and W(E₆) are excluded: their 6-dimensional representations are defined over ℚ, so the period rank is ≤ 10 | see §1.3 | a hypothesis in the sources; an argument in this matrix, confirmed by the reviewer in the ℚ-scope |

### 1.3 Step R7: why the real groups are excluded

The sources give two reasons, neither as a proof:

- SCREEN: "the requirement that the character field be exactly K";
- SCREEN-REV: "S₇ and W(E₆) (rational characters)".

So in the sources the exclusion is **part of the definition of the object**: a datum is required to have a representation whose character field is K. That is a hypothesis, and the corollary must state it.

**A remark added by this matrix (for the reviewer to confirm or reject).** The hypothesis can be replaced by an argument. If σ is defined over ℚ, then M_σ = M₀ ⊗_ℚ K with M₀ = Hom_G(σ_ℚ, H¹(C, ℚ)), a polarized rational Hodge structure of weight 1 and ℚ-dimension 8. The polarization is symplectic, because the invariant form on σ_ℚ is symmetric and the cup form is alternating. So the Hodge structure varies in the Siegel space of genus 4, of dimension 10, and the period rank is ≤ 10 < 16. **Scope: representations defined over ℚ with a symmetric invariant form**, which is the case of S₇ and W(E₆). For such σ the same count gives ≤ n(n + 1)/2 < n² for every n ≥ 2. Nothing is claimed for representations defined only over a larger real field: revision 1 stated that generalisation without proof, and it is withdrawn (§4.1). The argument is not in the log. It was confirmed in this ℚ-scope by the ChatGPT review of PR #44.

## 2. `HCG-ALG-G34-RANK2-LOCAL` (Δ27 addendum 2, item ii)

### 2.1 Statement

In the realization of G₃₄ of ledger §6.12, with h₀ normalized so that every reflecting root has norm 6: let g ∈ G₃₄ with rank(1 − g) = 2 and E = im(1 − g). Then exactly one of the following holds.

| Type | Pointwise stabilizer of Fix(g) | Order of g | a = det(h₀\|E), root basis | b = det((1 − g)\|E) | a·b |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A₁ × A₁ | two commuting reflections | 2 | 36 | 4 | 144 = N(12) |
| A₂ | S₃, three reflections | 3 | 27 | 3 | 81 = N(9) |

In both cases [a·b] = [1].

### 2.2 Proof chain (LOC-v0.2 §5)

| Step | Content | Uses | State |
|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | The pointwise stabilizer P of Fix(g) is generated by the reflections of G₃₄ whose roots lie in E | Steinberg's fixed-point theorem (Steinberg 1964, Thm 1.5; Lehrer 2004, Thm 1.1) | **import**, classical and published. The source note records a limited reading of these papers; not re-read here |
| S2 | The 126 computed reflections are all the reflections of G₃₄ | the classical count of 126 reflections (cited from a table in Bessis); the realization of §6.12 is G₃₄ (order 39,191,040 on 756 minimal vectors) | **import** for the count; `HCG-ALG-G34A` for the realization |
| S3 | For two distinct root lines, \|h₀(v, w)\|² ∈ {0, 9} | exhaustive: 7875 pairs | `[ALG cert]`, two independent censuses |
| S4 | A complex 2-plane contains at most 3 root lines. With q unit roots in the plane, the Gram matrix has rank ≤ 2 and trace q, so q²/2 ≤ tr(Gram²) ≤ q + q(q − 1)/4, hence q ≤ 3. A plane with an orthogonal pair has exactly 2 | S3; linear algebra | proved in the note; confirmed by the census (2835 planes with 2 lines, 1680 with 3) |
| S5 | P has rank 2, so E contains 2 or 3 root lines: P = A₁ × A₁ or A₂. An element of P with no fixed vector in E is −1 on E, or a rotation of order 3 | S1, S4; g ∈ P | — |
| S6 | The table of §2.1: A₁ × A₁: Gram = diag(6, 6), 1 − g = 2. A₂: det Gram = 36 − 9 = 27, det(1 − g) = (1 − ω)(1 − ω²) = 3 | direct | exact, both censuses |

### 2.3 Hypotheses and limits

- The statement is about **one group in one realization**. It is carried to an arbitrary datum with image G₃₄ by §3.2, step C2.
- It classifies rank-2 elements **through Steinberg's theorem**. Nobody enumerated the 39,191,040 elements. The censuses enumerate root pairs and planes.
- The auditor did not match the realized group to its Shephard–Todd label by catalogue (ledger §2, OPEN 1 closure note); the identification rests on the study's own construction.

## 3. `HCG-COR-D6-NONSPLIT-NOGO` (Δ27 addendum 2, item iii)

### 3.1 Statement

Let (G, σ; g_j) be a datum with d = 6 that meets the target of §0. No condition on the character field is needed: the two rational groups of rank 6 are excluded by §1.3. Then

  [det(ϑS)] = [1],

where S is the form of `HCG-THM-PARABOLIC-DISC-LOCALITY`. This holds on every Hurwitz component with these local classes.

**Reading for polarized abelian varieties (a separate, conditional sentence).** If, in addition, the class of S is the discriminant of the induced polarized factor (Severn v2 Prop 7.1, unrefereed) and Weil's criterion is used, then no d = 6 datum gives a nonsplit Weil eightfold.

### 3.2 Proof chain

| Step | Content | Uses | State of what is used |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | The image is G₃₄, with twenty reflections, or eighteen reflections and one rank-2 element | §1 (R1–R7) | this matrix §1 |
| C2 | The class may be computed in the realization of §6.12 with the norm-6 form. Two K-forms of an absolutely irreducible representation with the same character are isomorphic over K (Noether–Deuring), and h₀ is unique up to q ∈ ℚ_{>0}. A change of h₀ by q multiplies Πa_j by q^R with R = 20, a norm. A change of basis in E_j multiplies a_j by a norm and leaves b_j unchanged | representation theory; locality (a_j, b_j are defined through E_j) | classical; the scale invariance is in LOC-v0.2 §4. **The Noether–Deuring step is added by this matrix** |
| C3 | A reflection s of G₃₄ has a(s) = 6, b(s) = 2, a·b = 12 | direct | exact |
| C4 | N_eff = 20: [det(ϑS)] = [(+1)·12²⁰] = [1] | locality with n = 4; C3 | §6.24 (a) |
| C5 | N_eff = 19: [det(ϑS)] = [12¹⁸·144] or [12¹⁸·81], both [1] | locality; §2 | §6.24 (a); this matrix §2 |
