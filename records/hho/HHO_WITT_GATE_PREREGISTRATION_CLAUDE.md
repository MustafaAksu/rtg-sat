## 1. The quantity

For a tree T of the base and the σ-part of the cover, with the hypotheses of `HHO_D_SOURCE_READING_CLAUDE.md` §4.2:

  w(T) = Σ_{internal edges e} dim V^{⟨g_e⟩} − Σ_{vertices v} dim V^{H_v}  (V^G = 0),

H_v generated by the elements on the three edges at v. Fixed-space dimensions depend only on the representation, not on h₀.

## 2. The family of trees (fixed now)

- **Planar family.** For a datum with its recorded marking (g₁, …, g_N), all trivalent trees whose internal edges cut the cyclic order 1, …, N into two arcs ("interval trees"). For N = 20 there are Catalan(18) = 477 638 700 of them. The maximum and minimum of w over the family are computed **exactly** by dynamic programming over intervals, rooted at leaf N. The boundary product of an arc is the product of its elements in the recorded order.
- **For N = 4** the planar family has 2 of the 3 trees. The third is added through the marking (g₁, g₂g₃g₂⁻¹, g₂, g₄) obtained by one braid move.
- **For W5, W6, W7** the tree of the recorded (H0)–(H4) certificate is also evaluated directly, without the dynamic programme.
- No other tree and no other marking is used. In particular, the planar family is **not** every tree in the braid orbit for N = 20.

## 4. What a result would mean

- **A nonsplit datum with w = 4 on some tree of the family** would contradict the Witt bound. Then at least one of the following is wrong: the formula of §1 or its geometric hypotheses; locality; bridge B1; the recorded class; or the program. It would be reported as such and investigated, not explained away.
- **A split datum with w < 4 on its certificate tree** would contradict the recorded Hodge–Tate certificate, and would point at the formula or the program.
- **All predictions met:** consistency on these data. It says nothing about (D), and nothing about trees outside the family.

## 5. Checks of the program (fixed now)

1. Product one is asserted for every marking, in the matrix convention used.
2. The dynamic programme is compared with an explicit enumeration of all planar trees on random sub-data with N ≤ 9 taken from the same groups.
3. The witness tree of each maximum is rebuilt and evaluated directly.
4. For W5–W7 the certificate tree is evaluated without the dynamic programme.
5. Exact arithmetic over ℚ(ω); standard library only; `python3 -I`; no HCG engine and no earlier HHO script is imported (input data are read from the files above or copied from them with their source named).
