**Observations (no prediction was made):**
- G(6,1,6) Q0 and Q2, class [1]: max = min = **4** on the planar family. So within one group, at one dimension and with the same count N_eff = 20, the split data reach 4 and the nonsplit data stop at 3.
- C₃ × F₂₀, the 27 tuples of class [1] and signature (4,4): the maximum over the family is 0, 1, 2, 3 or 4. Counts by (N, max): (4,0): 4, (4,1): 6, (4,2): 5, (4,4): 1, (5,1): 3, (5,2): 3, (5,3): 3, (5,4): 2. So **a split datum need not reach 4 on a small family**. The remark in `HHO_D_SOURCE_READING_CLAUDE.md` §4.5 that "max over trees equals the Witt index" on three C₆ data does not extend to these data. They have four or five branch points, and the family is the planar one; nothing more is claimed.
- On W5–W7 and on all five G(6,1,6) data, w is the same for every planar tree of the recorded marking. I have no explanation for this. All these data consist of reflections (c_j = 1).

## 2. What it shows, and what it does not

- **Shows:** on seven nonsplit data of three different groups (G(6,1,6) with N = 20; C₃ × F₂₀ with N = 4) no tree of the family reaches w = 4; on the three split G₃₄ certificates, and on two split G(6,1,6) data, w = 4. This is the behaviour the Witt bound requires if the dual-graph formula, locality, bridge B1 and the recorded classes are all right. A failure would have shown that one of them is wrong; none occurred.
- **Does not show:** anything about (D); anything about trees outside the planar family of the recorded marking (for N = 20 the braid orbit has many more trees); that w = 4 is reached by every split datum; that the dual-graph formula holds (its geometric hypotheses are taken as stated in the reading note §4.2).
- The recorded classes were taken from the records, not recomputed.

## 3. Method and checks

- **Quantity:** w(T) = Σ_{internal edges} dim V^{⟨g_e⟩} − Σ_{vertices} dim V^{H_v}, with H_v generated by the two interval products at the vertex. Fixed spaces only; h₀ is not used.
- **Family:** all planar trees of the recorded marking, rooted at the last leaf, by an exact dynamic programme over intervals; for N = 4 also the third tree, through one braid move. The certificate trees of W5–W7 are evaluated directly.
- **Data:** W5–W7 rebuilt from the root vectors of the HCG certificates (20 reflections s₁, s₁, …, s₁₀, s₁₀); G(6,1,6) rebuilt from the definitions in `evidence/hho/code/g616.py` (not imported); C₃ × F₂₀ read from `evidence/hho/outputs/a4_direct_out.txt` (convention ρ(f)e_x = e_{f(x)}; product one holds in the recorded order).
- **Checks, all passed:** product one for every marking; the dynamic programme equals explicit enumeration of all planar trees on 40 random product-one markings (N = 4 to 9) for each of the three groups; every extremal witness tree re-evaluated directly; 300 random planar trees of W5 and of P1 evaluated directly (values 4 and 3 only).
- **Files:** `evidence/hho/d-witt/witt_gate_planar_claude.py` (SHA-256 `656fff29ada8470b06958514271c0f1505202f4840f845171fbbbed85d09839a`) and its output (`315580fe64681813ce6b5b3deb8f2dbbf14660d1e59a1112eee90b12bf41cac6`), listed in `evidence/hho/d-witt/SHA256SUMS`. Run from the repository root: `python3 -I evidence/hho/d-witt/witt_gate_planar_claude.py`, about 50 seconds, Python 3.13.18, standard library; replayed byte-identically.
