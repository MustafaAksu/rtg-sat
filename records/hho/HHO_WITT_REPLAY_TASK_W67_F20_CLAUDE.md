**Date:** 18 Nov 2026. **Level:** `[SESSION]` task description. It adopts nothing and changes no grade. It was written on the PI instruction of 18 Nov 2026 ("2 de yapabildiklerimizi yapalım": do what can be done on the second priority). Addressed to the ChatGPT HHO owner session.

**Why.** In `hho-v0.5`, register B.8 has three numerical rows. `HHO-NUM-WITT-C6` and `HHO-NUM-WITT-PLANAR` are CHECKED by two implementations. **`HHO-NUM-WITT-PLANAR-SINGLE` is PROPOSED: only Claude's program computed it.** This task asks for an independent replay. If the replay agrees, the row can be raised to CHECKED (two implementations) in a later edition. That change is a PI decision, not part of this task. The task does not depend on the open LIT request (`HHO-OPEN-WITT-SOURCE`).

**Definitions:**
- w(T) = Σ_{internal edges e} dim V^{⟨g_e⟩} − Σ_{vertices v} dim V^{H_v} + dim V^G.
- g_e is the product of the leaves on one side of e, in the order of the marking.
- H_v is the group generated by the products on the three edges at v.
- In every datum here V^G = 0. This should be asserted, not assumed.

The values are public, so the replay is not blind. **Its independence is in the code:** none of the following may be imported or copied:
- `witt_gate_planar_claude.py`;
- `hho_witt_w5_exact_independent.py`;
- HCG's engines.

A method different from Claude's interval dynamic programme over exact ℚ(ω) matrices is welcome but not required. Examples:
- a dynamic programme of your own design;
- direct enumeration for N ≤ 5;
- fixed spaces computed modulo a split prime, with the exact rank confirmed.
