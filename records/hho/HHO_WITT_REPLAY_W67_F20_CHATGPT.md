Separately rebuild the exact comb certificate from the source intervals and evaluate it. A full-input local replay is still an outstanding reproducibility step, not a mathematical discrepancy.

**F20.** Completely different algorithm: in the finite group `C3 x AGL(1,5)`, compute each subgroup generated by vertex/edge monodromies by exact closure. For the coefficient representation `V=omega^k (Perm_5 - 1)`, obtain `dim V^H` by counting affine `H`-orbits with twist-trivial stabilizers, minus the trivial-character multiplicity. Independently check every dimension by the exact character-average identity `(1/|H|) sum_H omega^k (fixed_points(h)-1)` in `Z[omega]`. Enumerate the two planar `N=4` bracketings plus the third topology from the explicit braid `(g1, g2*g3*g2^{-1}, g2, g4)`, **once each**, and all five `N=5` planar bracketings. Assert product one, group order 60 and `V^G=0` for **40/40** source tuples. This Python run used both byte-exact original F20 source files and verified their full SHA-256.
