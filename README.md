# rtg-sat — RTG satellite studies

Public scientific research repository for studies that are **inspired by RTG or related to it, but outside the RTG programme's scope**. The studies' own research, code, outputs and records are public here; third-party and licence-restricted sources are not stored here (they stay in the private `rtg-lit`), see `CONVENTIONS.md` §4. RTG itself lives in the public repository `MustafaAksu/rtg-corpus`. This repository holds no RTG result and is not part of the RTG canon.

- Which studies are here, their owners and state: `REGISTRY.md`.
- How a study works here, and how it relates to `rtg-corpus` and `rtg-lit`: `CONVENTIONS.md`.
- Cross-study inboxes: `pipes/`.

PI: Mustafa (final authority on all scientific and methodological decisions).
