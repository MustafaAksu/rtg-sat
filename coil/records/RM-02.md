# V. What this record does NOT support

1. **Any cross-layer mapping.** All instances are in the physical layer.
2. **Merging the archival and experimental families.** Charter §9.0: different lines, no named bridge. .e's fracture does **not** fill .a1's uncalibrated upper boundary.

"Window" is not one object.

6. **`[FALSIFIED]` (RUN-MM1-PAIRBC v1.0.1, P-κ)** ~~that the β_c(frac) numbers are invariant~~ — measured: as a rises 5.2×, β_c falls 4.92× (tri0) / 4.79× (path3). Pair A's 35–88 range holds only at a = 0.03; the table was derived from a single a point.

## V.2 Continuous counterpart and ontological status `[OPEN — the programme's highest-leverage question]`
