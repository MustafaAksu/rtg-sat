**The feasibility target must be sharpened.** Condition (L) is not an intrinsic property of a polarized eightfold or its Hodge filtration. It is a property of a Hurwitz transfer datum `(G, sigma, local conjugacy classes)` and admissible cyclic supplier characters: `e_sigma` must lie in the subgroup of the relevant centralizer-character lattice generated by supplier characters (Severn Definition 4.5). Likewise, (D) requires a period map of rank 16. Thus an arbitrary explicit PEL family with a nonsplit discriminant is not by itself a solution.

## 2. [SESSION — ALG] Existence at the PEL level is elementary

Let `K=Q(omega)`, `omega^2+omega+1=0`. On `V=K^8`, take

```
h_ns = diag(1, 1, 1, 1, -1, -1, -1, -2).
```

The Hermitian signature is (4,4), and `det h_ns = 2`. Since the prime 2 is inert in Q(omega), every nonzero field norm has even 2-adic valuation; hence `2` is not a norm. The discriminant class `[2]` is nonsplit. The positive subspaces have an explicit 16-dimensional chart: with `D=diag(1,1,1,2)`,

```
U_+(Z) = {(x, Zx): x in C^4},  Z in Mat_4(C),  I_4-Z* D Z > 0.
```

This is the standard PEL period-domain chart: `U_+(Z)` and its Hermitian-orthogonal complement specify the two Weil eigenspaces and hence the weight-one Hodge filtration (with the usual homology/cohomology dual convention). With a suitable `O_K`-lattice and integral multiple of the alternating form, this gives polarized eightfolds of Weil type, exactly as in Severn v2 §2.1.

**Scope:** This establishes explicit Hodge filtrations and a nonsplit (4,4) family of full dimension 16. It does **not** establish (L): no finite-cover datum or suppliers have been given. Nor does it give a new proof of algebraicity of Weil classes.

## 3. [SESSION — EXACT ALG] A genuine algebraic covering family meeting the first two conditions

Consider the **smooth projective normalizations** of the degree-6 cyclic covers of `P^1` with pairwise distinct branch parameters `t_1,...,t_10`:

```
C_t : y^6 = [prod_{i=1}^5 (x-t_i)^2] (x-t_6)^3
                 [prod_{i=7}^9 (x-t_i)^4] (x-t_10)^5.
```

The exponents sum to 30, divisible by 6, so infinity is unbranched; gcd(6,2,3,4,5)=1 ensures connectedness. Riemann–Hurwitz gives `g(C_t)=15`. Let the deck generator act by `y -> zeta_6 y`. The faithful primitive part of its Jacobian is an eight-dimensional abelian factor with `K=Q(zeta_6)=Q(omega)`.

The two 1-form eigenspaces (up to convention for which K-embedding is called sigma) are explicitly:

```
F^1_(zeta6^-1) = span_C { x^j dx/y : j = 0,1,2,3 }
F^1_(zeta6^-5) = span_C { x^j prod_i(x-t_i)^(a_i-1) dx/y^5 : j = 0,1,2,3 }.
```

At each finite branch point the local uniformizing parameter shows these have nonnegative valuation; at infinity the valuation of the j-th form is `3-j`. The Chevalley–Weil counts are 4 and 4: `sum_i a_i/6 = 5` and `sum_i {5 a_i/6} = 5`. Consequently the primitive factor has signature (4,4) and its `F^1` is explicitly represented by rational algebraic differentials.

**Exact discriminant computation.** Use Severn v2 Lemma 7.2 for the rank-one local system with monodromies `T_i=zeta_6^{a_i}`, and `theta=omega-omega_bar=1+2omega`. A K-basis of parabolic cocycles is obtained by fixing `c_9=0`, setting `c_k=1` for each `k=1,...,8` successively and all other free values zero, then solving `sum_i Q_i c_i=0` for `c_10`; `Q_i=prod_{j<i}T_j`. The quotient by coboundaries is legitimate because `T_9 != 1`. The K-Hermitian Gram matrix `h=theta S` satisfies

```
det_K h = 3/2,   [det_K h] = [2] in Q^x / Nm(K^x).
```

The exact script checks skew-Hermitian `S`, Hermitian `theta S`, determinant 3/2, closure, a second cocycle gauge, and an altered branch-point ordering. The passage from parabolic cup to the actual induced Jacobian polarization uses the standard finite-cover trace/cup identification formulated in Severn v2 Proposition 7.1; it changes the discriminant at most by a rational 8th-power norm or conjugation. This step is a **source-based geometric dependency**, not a new first-principles proof of Proposition 7.1.

**Nonsplit proof:** `3 = Nm(1-omega)`, and `(3/2)/2 = Nm((1-omega)/2)`. Thus `[3/2]=[2]`. As 2 is inert in K, `[2] != [1]`.

## 4. [SESSION — SOURCE DEFINITION + ALG] Why this explicit family fails (L)

`G=C_6` has subgroups only of orders 1, 2, 3, 6. For Severn's admissibility (Definition 4.1), supplier characters must have order >=3, even positive `h=2q-2+s`, and simple branch exponents. All possibilities are excluded:

| Subgroup and character | Cover and branch counts | Obstruction |
|---|---|---|
| `H'=C6`, primitive order-6 character | 10 exponents `2^5,3,4^3,5` | Not *simple*: exponents cannot be paired `a,-a` (in particular the exponent 3 occurs once) |
| `H'=C6`, order-3 character | quotient `C/C2 -> P1`, 9 effective branch points, `q=0`, `h=7` | h is odd |
| `H'=C3`, order-3 character | `C -> C/C3`; the intermediate degree-2 base has 2 branch points and genus `q=0`; the cyclic 3-cover has `s=17`, `h=15` | h is odd |
| `H'=C2` or trivial | No character of order >=3 | Not a supplier |

There is therefore no admissible supplier for this datum; the character subgroup `L` in Severn Definition 4.5 is zero, whereas `e_sigma` is nonzero. **(L) fails.** This is an explicit, falsifiable obstruction, not an inference from missing search hits.

Further, the base has only `N-3=7` moduli dimensions, so (D) at target rank 16 is impossible even if (L) were repaired. The example is a *feasibility separator*, not a successful transfer datum.

## 5. [SESSION — CONTROL] A split comparison with (L)

For `G=C3` and branch exponents `1^5,2^5`, the curve `y^3 = prod_{i<=5}(x-t_i) prod_{i>5}(x-t_i)^2` has genus 8, `K=Q(omega)` signature (4,4) and explicit differential eigenspaces. The faithful supplier `(C3,chi)` is admissible (five inverse pairs, `h=8`), and in this one-pair character lattice its supplier character is `e_sigma`, so **(L) holds**. The same exact cup computation gives `det h=1`, i.e. split discriminant. Its 7-dimensional base cannot satisfy (D).

| Family | Explicit algebraic F1 | Signature (4,4) | Disc nonsplit | (L) | Period rank 16 feasible? |

| Full PEL domain with `diag(1^4,-1^3,-2)` | YES (explicit domain chart) | YES | YES `[2]` | Not defined without G/Hurwitz datum | YES in the identity PEL period coordinates; no algebraic supplier or cycle result |

| `C3` control (`1^5,2^5`) | YES | YES | NO, `[1]` | **YES** | NO (base dimension 7) |

## 6. Efficient next gate

**[SESSION — ALG]** For a genus-zero irreducible transfer representation of K-dimension `d` and target `dim_K H_par=8`, the Chevalley–Weil/parabolic dimension identity gives

```
sum_{j=1}^N rank_K(1-sigma(g_j)) = 8 + 2d.
```

Dominance of the 16-dimensional eightfold moduli family requires **at least 19 effective branch points**: `N_eff-3 >= 16`. Here "effective" means `sigma(g_j) != 1`. Branches in `ker sigma` cannot add variation to the sigma-factor: the factor is obtained from the quotient cover with group `G/ker sigma`, which is unramified at these extra marks. Thus `d>=6`. In the lowest `d=6` case, the effective rank budgets are only `N_eff=20` (twenty rank-one classes) or `N_eff=19` (eighteen rank-one plus one rank-two class).

**One-dimensional impossibility for (D).** For any genus-zero one-dimensional nontrivial `K`-character (`d=1`) with `dim_K H_par=8`, every effective local monodromy has rank one and therefore `N_eff=8+2=10`. Its family factors through the Hurwitz family of the cyclic image quotient with 10 effective marks and hence varies in dimension at most 7. In particular, **no genus-zero transfer datum with a one-dimensional target representation can dominate the 16-dimensional Weil-eightfold moduli**, even if arbitrarily many ineffective marks are appended. This is a dimension obstruction independent of the discriminant and of (L).

A cost-efficient search therefore filters candidate `G, sigma` and Nielsen-class data by **(a)** dimension/signature and branch-rank budget, **(b)** Severn (L) in its actual supplier-character lattice (or a genuine real-class theorem), **(c)** exact nonsplit discriminant computed from rank-8 parabolic cup, **before** any attempt to build expensive algebraic period matrices. Only if a candidate passes these three gates should one build an explicit `F1(t)` and test a `16 x 16` interior period-map Jacobian minor.

This first cycle has not produced a datum satisfying all requested properties; existence in the intended transfer/Hurwitz sense remains **OPEN**. It *has* produced a fully specified nonsplit eigencurve family and a separate (L)-positive split control with independently replayable exact determinants. The example does not reopen the blind auditor or modify any HCG adopted claim.
