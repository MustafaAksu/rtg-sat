**(ii) Clifford and Galois.**
- V|_Λ = U₁ ⊕ U₂ with τ₁ ≇ τ₂, because Ind τ₁ is irreducible exactly when τ₁ is not s-stable. So U₁, U₂ are the two Λ-isotypic components, and that decomposition is canonical.

**(iii) Reflection generation on Y.**
