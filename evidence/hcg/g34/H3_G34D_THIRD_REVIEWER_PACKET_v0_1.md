## 1. The question (narrow)

Claim under review: Severn, Hexagon 2610.00387v1, Proposition 4.2 ("rank at a maximal degeneration"), applied to the explicit witness W5 below. Does W5 give period-map rank 16 for a component of the Hurwitz space of G₃₄-covers of ℙ¹ branched at 20 points, with local monodromy in the reflection class?

**Do not presume the theorem is correct.** For each item below, return either a precise missing hypothesis, a counterexample, or a confirmation with references.

1. **Family and rigidification.** Construct the local 17-parameter marked Hurwitz family near the maximally degenerate point determined by the curve list. Determine any stabilizer, rigidification or level structure, and any finite or Kummer base change needed.
2. **Loops.** Identify the lift of each boundary loop (|q_j| small, arg q_j from 0 to 2π) as a signed full Dehn twist / Artin braid. In particular, treat curve [19,20] against its complement [1,18] when point 20 is placed at ∞ (Dettweiler–Wewers Assumption 1.2).
3. **Coefficient transport.** Determine the coefficient-torsor scalars χ(γ) in Dettweiler–Wewers Theorem 1.3 for these loops.
4. **Monodromy cone.** Is the actual monodromy logarithm of each loop ε·c_j·N_j with c_j > 0 and one common ε? State the exact hypotheses of the several-variable nilpotent orbit theorem (Schmid; Cattani–Kaplan–Schmid) as they apply here.
5. **Rank.** Given (4), do the data below force generic rank 16 of the period map of the σ-isotypic Prym–Tyurin family?

## 2. Exact data (verify these yourself; do not trust them)

**Group.** G₃₄ ⊂ GL₆(ℚ(ω)), ω² + ω + 1 = 0. It is generated by the 126 order-2 reflections r_v(x) = x − 2h(x,v)/h(v,v)·v, with h(x,y) = Σx_k ȳ_k, for v ranging over the 756 norm-6 vectors of the Eisenstein Coxeter–Todd lattice

  K₁₂ = {x ∈ ℤ[ω]⁶ : x_i ≡ x_j mod (1+2ω), Σx_i ≡ 0 mod 3}.

|G₃₄| = 39,191,040.
